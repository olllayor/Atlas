Rail groups by kind (local agent vs custom endpoint) instead of one flat list — the two have different configuration shapes, so grouping tells you that before you click.

Tabs only appear on providers that actually have two distinct concerns (config vs a real model list, like OpenCode). Custom endpoints stay single-scroll like today, since their model list is short and editable inline.

Disabled/unavailable providers (Codex CLI) sit in the list greyed out with a one-line explanation instead of being hidden — so people from the T3 screenshot don't wonder where it went.

No per-machine tabs (M2 / mac mini in the T3 screenshot) — Atlas settings are already per-device, that concept doesn't map here.
